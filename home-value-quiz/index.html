Where the numbers come from

This demo runs on one fixed snapshot: AMPI San Miguel MLS asking prices, pulled on 21 Nov 2026 and summarised into price-per-m² ranges by area and size. No single listing is shown or stored.

They are asking prices, not sale prices. A quiz result starts a conversation. It is not an appraisal.

A live pull needs the permission of the API key holder. That is usually the office whose MLS or IDX key is used. With that permission, the pull runs on a regular schedule, for example every Monday, and your quiz stays current without any manual work.
