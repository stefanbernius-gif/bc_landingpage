01 · Executive summary
The strongest brand in the market has its weakest funnel

Where you stand

The Client is one of the top-ranked teams in the US by volume, verified by an independent industry ranking, with about 90K Instagram followers and more ranking keywords than any home-market team we measured.

What is broken

  • Contact paths break. The call link on the team lead's own profile page dials a nine-digit number, the Resort B office link rings another office, and four common web addresses end on an error page. Fixed since we flagged them: the fake blog number (24 Sep, the same day), the dead ‘Browse By Market’ button and the Instagram bio link (both by 16 Oct).

  • Nothing is traced. No report links a lead to its source, so no one can see which marketing brings in closings.

A 90-day plan with three checkpoints. The first — tracking live, leaks fixed — takes 14 days and is mostly settings, not a rebuild.

Standards this audit measures against
  • Page speed: Google Core Web Vitals. LCP 2.5 s or faster, INP 200 ms or faster, CLS 0.1 or lower, for 75% of real visits (web.dev).
  • Email authentication: SPF (RFC 7208, at most 10 DNS lookups), DKIM, and DMARC (RFC 7489). Since February 2024, Google and Yahoo require SPF, DKIM and a DMARC record from bulk senders.
  • Search: Google Search Central. 301 redirects for moved pages, one H1 with headings in order, a unique meta description per page, an XML sitemap, and JSON-LD structured data (Schema.org RealEstateAgent).
  • Local search: the same name, address and phone (NAP) on the site, Google Business Profile and directories. Reviews are asked for without incentives, per Google's review policy.
  • Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 level AA, for example tap targets of at least 24 × 24 px (SC 2.5.8).
  • Consent: TCPA consent wording for calls and texts, and CAN-SPAM opt-out rules for marketing email.
  • Revenue anchor: the team's own independently verified 2025 sales data.
